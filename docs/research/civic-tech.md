# What works in budget transparency (researched 2026-09-26)

## Evidence
| Study | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Reinikka & Svensson, Uganda | Publishing each school's grant in the newspapers cut capture from about 80% to under 20%. | https://econ.lse.ac.uk/staff/rburgess/eea/svenssonjeea.pdf |
| Banerjee et al., Indonesia | Mailing each household a card stating its entitlement raised what households received by 26%. | https://www.nber.org/papers/w20923 |
| Olken, Indonesian roads | A credible audit threat worked; village meetings alone barely helped. | https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/517935 |
| Björkman & Svensson, Uganda | Community monitoring with facilitated meetings and follow-up improved outcomes. | https://academic.oup.com/qje/article-abstract/124/2/735/1905094 |
| Andhra Pradesh social audits | Detection went up, but without punishment theft shifted elsewhere. | https://www.isid.ac.in/~fafridi/doc/ipf.pdf |

## Models
| Model | Lesson | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BudgIT Tracka (Nigeria) | Project-level tracking with photos and town halls got 47 of 319 abandoned projects restarted. | https://budgit.org/our_programs/tracka/ |
| Kenya county budget scorecards | Publicly ranking disclosure raised average scores from 41 to 56. | https://internationalbudget.org/publications/kenyas-county-budget-transparency-survey-2023/ |
| Indonesia Dana Desa billboards | Totals that are too aggregated amount to "pseudo-transparency". | https://www.kompas.id/artikel/en-transparansi-semu-pengelolaan-dana-desa |

## Design rules we applied
- Itemised entitlements, and "per household" figures.
- A disclosure scorecard with one-tap RTI requests.
- Volunteer verification entries that carry a date and photos.
- Posters and QR codes for offline reach.
- Bangla digits with lakh/crore, a small page weight, and offline support.

Risks to guard against:
- Never publish beneficiary NIDs or names. See the Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025.
- Use neutral wording, because of cyber-law risk.
- Keep the site clearly independent and unofficial.
