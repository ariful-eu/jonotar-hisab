## UP Act 2009, key sections
| Section | What it says |
|---|---|
| s.4 | The ward shava is made up of all voters on the ward's list. |
| s.5 | At least 2 meetings a year, one of them annual. Quorum is 5%. Notice must be given 7 days ahead. The ward member reports on the previous year's work and spending. |
| s.6 | The ward shava sets project priorities and makes the final beneficiary priority list, which the UP cannot change without proven irregularity (6(7)). Budget and purchase figures go on a public board in the ward (6(2)). Audit reports go to the ward shava (6(3)). |
| s.7(4) | Spending without the ward shava's approval is the personal liability of whoever spent it. |
| s.57 | The budget is due at least 60 days before 1 July, presented at a public budget session. A copy goes to the UNO, who may correct it within 30 days. |
| s.58 | Final accounts are presented at the open session and sent to the UNO within 60 days. |
| s.49–50 | A citizen charter is required, and services must also be published online (ICT). |
| s.65–66 | Taxes are set under the model tax schedule. |
| s.78–80 | Right to information. Delay costs the official Tk 50/day; refusing or giving false information costs at least Tk 1,000. |

## RTI Act 2009
- **s.8:** apply on Form ক or plain paper.
- **s.9:** answer within 20 working days (30 if several units are involved); life/death cases within 24 hours. A refusal must come within 10 working days with reasons.
- **s.24:** appeal within 30 days; the appeal is decided in 15 days.
- **s.25:** complain to the Information Commission within 30 days.
- **s.27:** fine of Tk 50/day.
- **To verify:** the copy fee (about Tk 2 per page) and the appellate authority for the UP (likely the UNO).

## Fees
Birth/death registration (https://orgbdr.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/69cf48059d736d71f1a34c8c):
- Free within 45 days; Tk 25 from 45 days to 5 years; Tk 50 after that.
- Correcting the date of birth costs Tk 100; other corrections Tk 50; a duplicate costs Tk 50.

Trade licence and certificate fees vary by union and should be in the citizen charter.

## Evidence of common violations
- **TIB National Household Survey 2023:** 44.2% of households using local government institutions experienced corruption; 29.7% paid bribes (average Tk 884). For certificates, 64.5% paid bribes. https://www.ti-bangladesh.org/images/2023/report/nhs/NHS-2023-Executive-Summary-En.pdf
