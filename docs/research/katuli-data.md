# Katuli Union: what is published (researched 2026-09-26)

- **Official portal:** https://katuliup.tangail.gov.bd/ (mostly unchanged since 2014–2016).
- **Budget:** the only one online is FY2014-15. It is the open-budget meeting minutes, last updated 8 Dec 2014: https://katuliup.tangail.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/69959c8535ce18e1c0789db4
  - The text contradicts itself: it gives two meeting dates (28/05/2014 and 30/05/2014) and two different chairs, one of them the chairman of Bahertoil UP. It was probably copied from another union's template.
  - Re-transcribed line by line on 2026-09-27. The income line items add up exactly to the stated totals (৯৪,৫৮,০৫৮ / ৪৫,৪৭,৩৮৬ / ৮৩,৮৭,৬০৪).
  - The minutes record that tax collectors had been paid 25% commission, and that the meeting cut it to the legal 20%.
- **Projects page:** https://katuliup.tangail.gov.bd/pages/field-projects lists 9 rows with no dates and almost no amounts. Two rows contain only website boilerplate.
- **Empty sections:** notices, tenders, citizen charter, tax schedule, and current beneficiary lists. The old-age and widow pages hold partial 2014 name lists; we do not republish those names.
- **Facts:**
  - 26.92 km², 9 wards, 24 villages.
  - Census 2022: 32,540 people and 7,884 households (BBS). The 2011 figures were 29,811 people and 6,433 households.
- **Leadership:**
  - The portal officers page lists Chairman Md. Iqbal Hossain and Secretary Mahbubur Rahman. It is undated and unverified for 2026.
  - UP elections are reportedly planned for January 2027.
- **News:** no reporting found on corruption or projects in Katuli. The only item is the anniversary of the "Jagrat Katuli Union" social group (Sep 2026).
